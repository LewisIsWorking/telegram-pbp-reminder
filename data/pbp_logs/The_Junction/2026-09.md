The Junction.

Acomhal is a trade hub, a small city in the Eel Sea.

Acomhal is the name for "junction" in the language of glass.

This is the  place where Paths and peoples meet.

Many species live and trade in Acomhal.

Many factions compete for the hubs large trade network.

Gloine Mhargadh, the Glass Market, trades in Vitreous.

Na hIarannaigh, the Iron Ones, run the city's machines.

An Fás, the Growth, grows living goods.

Clann Deamhan, the Demon Clan, keep to their own enclave.

Na Siopadóirí, the Shopkeepers, are small independent traders.

Comhairle Acomhal, the Council, governs the city.

The Council is meant to be neutral. It is slow to act and ineffective, only rarely delivering upon their politcal mandate.

Every faction drinks at An Crosbhealach, the Crossroads. 
It is neutral ground.

Lately, shipments into Acomhal arrive poisoned or destroyed.
Every faction blames another.

This is where your story begins.
